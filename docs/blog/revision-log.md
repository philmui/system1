## Disposition of adversarial findings

The plugin's first material finding was temporal feature leakage: partitioning entire conversations cannot prevent later turns from leaking into an earlier decision. Version 03 introduced timestamped input snapshots, distinct outcome labels, and the same boundary in the pipeline. These controls remain in versions 07–09.

The second plugin review found that the illustration allowed offline evaluation to lead directly to rollout, contradicting the controlled service pilot required by version 06. Version 07 references `training-pipeline-final.svg`, which shows offline qualification followed by a randomized pilot and rollout only after pilot gates pass. The earlier SVG remains available, preserving the earlier drafts. The final caption, accessible description, and notes describe the same sequence.

Independent reviews additionally tightened identity and authorization boundaries, completion semantics, cost accounting, statistical comparisons, and feedback labeling. The final editorial check found no blocking issue. Its optional suggestion to simplify the statistical-unit wording was accepted before saving version 07. The opening's repeated short component sentences were also removed because the table and architecture figure explain the same allocation more clearly.

Version 08's independent content review identified a missing-evidence ambiguity in the conditional-return example and an ambiguous cost denominator. The article now requires verified repair/replacement-status facts before frontier interpretation and consistently measures operating cost per incoming case. Its confidence statement distinguishes TypeSafe's documented distribution-concentration definition from the recommendation to validate correctness locally.

The pictorial review checked the revised artwork against the article. The fallback detour now names both `unclear` and `complex` inputs, and unresolved human review has a visible hold exit. All three canonical SVG paths now display the pictures described by the captions. The [pictorial logic review](../diagrams/returns-pictorial-logic-review.md) records both findings as resolved. The separate [Codex plugin review](../diagrams/returns-pictorial-codex-review.txt) completed with an `approve` verdict and no material findings across the three rendered illustrations, blog 08, and four included teaching slides. Its scope and the artifact checks are documented in [verification](verification.md).

## Suggestions deliberately adapted or declined

- Gait geometry, health conditions, JEPA, and numbered notebooks were excluded as unrelated to the requested Salesforce article and unsupported by supplied data. Their methodological intent is represented in the evaluation design.
- Dataset preservation means an immutable source snapshot, preserved relationships, and recorded transformations. No unchanged-shape or unchanged-distribution claim was made.
- The drafts do not transplant the LangChain litigation example, TypeSafe SDK, Jev performance claims, or a vendor training objective into Salesforce terminology.
- Executable AgentScript integration code and training recipes were omitted because the public material does not establish a `koa-action` adapter or training API. The proposed application contracts make the design concrete without presenting invented product behavior.
- Recommended word reductions were applied where they removed repetition. Essential method details were retained to meet the user's requested rigor.
- The statistical question changed from latency-centered evaluation in early drafts to reliability-constrained cost reduction after the user's explicit theme clarification. Latency remains an acceptance constraint.

## Version 09: a shared branching story

The article and slide 05 now follow the same request: exchange the headphones and return a separately purchased cable. The kit-component illustration deliberately uses a different purchased-unit relationship. Authentication is an authoritative service branch, and sign-in returns to its check. The two main illustrations separate the shared front desk from semantic task routing, keeping detailed recovery and policy rules in the notes and article.

The frontier path is available for residual interpretation after useful clarification and the applicable evidence checks. Missing stock, denied identity, absent policy or an unsupported handler follows its own configured route. Human review returns validated tasks through the same supported-combination mapping. AND tasks all remain obligations; OR/IF alternatives execute only when selected or triggered. The evaluation section uses the same distinction when counting unfinished cases.

The revision preserves the training and controlled-pilot protocol. Its main addition is a more precise model/workflow boundary, not an unperformed result. Review findings and artifact checks for this revision are recorded in [verification](verification.md).
