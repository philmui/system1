# Atlas access policy — 2025

Fictional policy for a synthetic educational example.

## Access duration

The 2025 access policy grants visiting researchers access for 30 days.
An extension requires approval by the project lead.

## Access approval

The 2025 access policy requires project lead approval before access begins.
Researchers use an individual account and complete an access log on each visit.

## Records

Access records are retained for 90 days. This policy took effect on January 1, 2025.
