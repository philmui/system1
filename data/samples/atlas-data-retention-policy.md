# Atlas data retention policy

Synthetic internal policy. Effective date: 2026-01-20.
Applies to everyone working on Project Atlas.

## Retention rules

Retain approved Atlas project reports for three years after project completion.
Delete temporary working exports within 30 days after an approved final report is stored.
Invoices follow the finance team's separate retention schedule.

## Ownership

The project steward reviews access every quarter and records exceptions in the policy register.
