# Atlas Northstar service agreement NSA-01

Synthetic agreement between Atlas Research Cooperative and Northstar Services.
Effective date: 2026-01-05. This agreement is signed by both fictional parties.

## Services

Northstar will operate the Atlas research platform for USD 2,400 per month.

## Termination

Either party may terminate the Atlas Northstar service agreement with 30 calendar days of written notice.
Fees remain payable for services delivered before the termination date.

## Entire agreement

Changes require a written amendment signed by both parties. Internal summaries do not amend this agreement.
